# Globe geography

Natural Earth land polygons at 1:110m scale. The source data is in the public domain.

- [Terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- [Source GeoJSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson), obtained on 15 November 2026.
- Source SHA-256: `9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9`.
- Transformation: numeric rings from Polygon/MultiPolygon geometries only, with validated coordinates rounded to three decimal places. Includes 127 polygons and 5,143 coordinates.

The renderer draws a stylized sphere in graphite and amber. Its shading and grid do not represent scientific measurements. This globe is independent of the NASA image-sonification panel.
