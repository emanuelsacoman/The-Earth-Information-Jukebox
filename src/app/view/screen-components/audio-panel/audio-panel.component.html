Method, credits and limitations

We scan 24 columns of eight regions, top to bottom, then left to right. Relative brightness is the mean of 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B over sampled pixels, divided by 255. The printed year is masked. Transparent or masked pixels are excluded.

Normalized brightness n selects one of 16 C-major pentatonic notes, C3 to C6: index = round(15 n). Frequency = 440 × 2^((MIDI − 69)/12) Hz. Voice gain = 0.06 + 0.14 n. Stereo pan = 2 x − 1, with x the horizontal position. Sine notes last 0.18 s, starting every 0.24 s at 1×. Speed changes timing and preserves pitch.
